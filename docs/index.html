minesplit · ZECATHON 2026 · Shielded Payments

Pay everyone privately. Prove they got their share.

A treasury pays all its recipients in one shielded Zcash transaction, then publishes a statement anyone can check. You are about to check the real one, from Zcash mainnet, right here in your browser. You will not see a single recipient.

The promise

The rule: half of everything the treasury receives goes to the people it pays. The other half keeps the service running. If there isn't enough for everyone's full amount, everyone gets the same share, so the treasury never overspends.

First user: Pedalshield, a private app that pays people to ride their bikes (built earlier, at the ZecHub Hackathon). minesplit itself was built from scratch for ZECATHON.

Who it's for

Anyone who pays many people and has to prove they paid fairly, without exposing who got what.

Grant programs & bounty boardsPay contributors without publishing each person's earnings.
Mining poolsPay miners privately and prove the pool took only its cut.
DAOs & co-opsShare revenue with members by a public rule, with private payouts.
PayrollPay a team without exposing salaries, and prove payroll was met.
Charities & aidReach people who could be at risk if identified, and prove donors' money arrived.
Reward appsLike Pedalshield: pay users for activity without building a record of them.

    What you are not seeing (on purpose)

    LeakHow it is closed
    Transparent addressesNone anywhere. Income and payouts are shielded; payout addresses are Orchard-only.
    Who was paid, and how much eachThe public statement is totals only. It refuses to publish an epoch with fewer than 5 payouts.
    Linking payouts to each otherA fresh address for every payout, from a random diversifier. Reuse is refused.
    TimingEveryone is paid in one batch per epoch.
    MemosOnly a random receipt id, like minesplit r=4dc7…
    Your IPAll wallet traffic goes over Tor.

    Our own wallet passes minesplit leak-report 6 of 6: no transparent outputs, all payouts Orchard/Ironwood, no address reuse, clean memos, one batched transaction, public file totals-only.
